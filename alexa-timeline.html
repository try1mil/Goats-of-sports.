2023-present

Joins the training group of renowned coach Eteri Tutberidze, wins the Junior World Championships, Wins the Junior Grand Prix Final.

Wins Skate Canada, Rostelecom Cup, qualifies for the Senior Grand Prix Finale and won bronze, wins bronze at the european championships.

Wins bronze in the world championships in Stockholm, wins Skate America and the U.S Classic, therefore establishes herself as one of the world's top senior skaters.

Wins the Olympic Silver Medal in women's singles, coming second, despite landing five quadruple jumps in her free skate, a historic achievement and becoming the first woman to land a quad at the olympic games.

Doesn't participate in competitions anymore, but shows up to domestic events and training sessions.
